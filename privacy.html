Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Flexo PDF (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application. Flexo PDF is a PDF utility app. We designed it to process documents on your device whenever possible.

This page is a template for transparency and store listing requirements. It is not legal advice. Review it before publishing, and update it when your practices change.

1. Who we are

Flexo PDF is developed by an independent developer. For privacy questions, contact: kamran.pervez13@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • Your PDF and image files are processed on your device for the tools you use (merge, compress, convert, edit, scan, and similar features).
  • We do not operate a Flexo PDF cloud account that uploads your documents to our servers for processing.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Development builds may keep diagnostic logs on the device. Those logs are not automatically sent to us from production builds today.

3. Information we process

3.1 Documents and media you choose

When you open, create, scan, convert, annotate, or export files, those files remain under your control on the device (and in locations you choose, such as Downloads or your photo library). We do not use the contents of your documents for advertising.

3.2 Device permissions

Depending on the feature and platform, the App may request:

  • Camera — for scan-related features (optional).
  • Photos / media / storage — to pick images or files and to save or share results.
  • Internet — for opening links (for example Privacy Policy, Terms, store pages, or sending feedback via your email app) and for future optional services described below.

You can deny optional permissions; some features may then be unavailable.

3.3 On-device diagnostics (development)

In development builds, the App may write local log files (for example app version, device model, OS version, and technical events) to help fix bugs. An “Export logs” control may appear only in development builds so a developer can share those files manually. Production builds are not intended to expose that export control.

3.4 Crash reporting (planned / optional)

We may later enable a crash-reporting service such as Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google). If enabled, the App may send anonymous technical crash data after a failure — for example stack traces, app version, device model, and OS version — so we can improve stability.

Crash reports are not intended to include your PDF contents, passwords, or full file paths. If we enable Crashlytics, we will update this Privacy Policy to state that it is active.

3.5 Feedback email

If you use “Send Feedback” or email us, your message is sent through your own email provider. We receive whatever you choose to include (for example your email address and message text).

4. How we use information

  • To provide PDF tools you request on your device
  • To save or share files you export
  • To respond to support messages you send
  • To improve reliability if crash reporting is enabled in the future

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. If Crashlytics or similar services are enabled, crash data is processed by the service provider (for example Google) under their terms and privacy policy, solely for crash analysis as configured in the App.

If you share a file through the system share sheet or open a link, the destination app or website handles that content under its own policy.

6. Data retention

Documents and local logs remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App (subject to OS behavior). Emails you send us are retained as needed to respond and for legitimate record-keeping.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

  • Revoke permissions in system settings
  • Delete exported files and uninstall the App
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy: kamran.pervez13@gmail.com

9. International users

If you use the App from outside the country where we operate, any limited information you send us (such as an email) may be processed where we or our providers are located.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
